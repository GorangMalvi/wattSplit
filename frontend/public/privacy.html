wattSplit privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

wattSplit splits a shared flat's electricity bill between flatmates. It is made and run by Gorang Malvi, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: Gorangmalvi@zohomail.com.

wattSplit has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and never sells data.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code, and to email you about payments and invites.
Names you enter for flatmates, and the household nameTo show who owes what.
Meter readings, bills, recharges and payments between flatmatesTo work out each person's bill and balance.
Email addresses you type to invite a flatmateTo send them the invite.
A voice recording, only when you tap Say itTo turn what you say into a payment for you to check. The recording is sent for transcription and is not kept by wattSplit.

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording a payment with Say it. It does not use your location, contacts, photos or other files.

Who can see it

Everything about a household is visible to its members: the people who have joined it with its code or an invite. Nobody else can see it.

Services that process it for us

ServiceWhat for
SupabaseDatabase and sign-in (servers in Singapore).
RenderRuns the wattSplit server (Singapore) and serves the website.
MailtrapSends sign-in codes and notification emails.
60dbTranscribes voice recordings into text, only when you use Say it.

The app downloaded from our website (not Google Play) also asks GitHub whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent.

All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. Payment and bill records belong to the whole household, so they are kept for the other members until the household is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap Account at the top, then Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in and email address and removes you from your households. If you are the only member of a household, that household and everything in it is deleted too. In a household you share, your name, readings and payments stay, because your flatmates' bills and balances depend on them. If you own a shared household, first make another member the owner (Household → Roommates → Make owner), or delete the household.

Without the app: see how to delete your account.

Children

wattSplit is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
