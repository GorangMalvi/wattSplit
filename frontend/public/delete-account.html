Delete your wattSplit account

wattSplit is made by Gorang Malvi. You can delete your account in either of these ways. You don't need the Android app.

1. On the website

  1. Open wattsplit.jugadu.tech and sign in with your email.
  2. Tap Account at the top.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

2. By email

Email Gorangmalvi@zohomail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my wattSplit account". It is done within 7 days and you get a reply when it is.

What is deleted

  • Deleted: your sign-in, your email address, your membership of every household, and every invite sent to your email.
  • Deleted: every household where you are the only member, with everything in it.
  • Kept: in a household you share, your name, meter readings and payments, because your flatmates' bills and balances depend on them. They are deleted when that household is deleted.

If you own a household other people have joined, make one of them the owner first (Household → Roommates → Make owner), or delete the household.
